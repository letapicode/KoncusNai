## Persistence, cancellation, and failure invariants

- Settings persistence remains outside the Workbench transaction; the window raises typed preference intents while `WorkbenchSettingsApplicationController` exclusively owns the in-window normalized/current/pending state and apply/recovery transaction.
- History stores are reached only through `WorkbenchHistoryController` and the existing query/command coordinators. Chat send and dictation recording receive typed persistence delegates from that boundary.
- `WorkbenchOperationSession`, `WorkbenchChatOperationSession`, `LatestOperationSession`, history coordinators, and `WorkbenchSpeechSession` cancel before releasing dependent resources and expose completion that their owners await during shutdown.
- The window initiates all owned disposals once; controller/session disposals are idempotent. Window/controller subscriptions are detached before dependency disposal. View timers are stopped and detached, and deferred view/window dispatcher operations are aborted or guarded before controller shutdown.
- Controllers translate expected cancellation into cancellation/stopped outcomes. Technical exception details go to `IDiagnostics`; user statuses are bounded and actionable. In WP-04, read-aloud playback was corrected so the device exception message is diagnostic-only.

## WP-04 findings and size judgment

WP-04 removed the obsolete `ApplyChatControlState`, `RefreshOperationalStatusVisibility`, `ApplyViewModel`, and `ApplyWorkbenchActionState` wrappers and collapsed their consecutive call sites to direct `RenderPresentation` calls. It also removed the transcript renderer's independent sidebar-action calculation, leaving the reducer as the single enablement/visibility projection. The unused `AppendComposerText` helper was deleted.

WP-04 added deterministic disposal for the composer expansion dispatcher operation and chat-progress timer, prevented a disposed operational-status view from restarting its timer, made the read-aloud dispatcher handoff replaceable/abortable, and stopped raw playback exception messages from reaching status UI. Tests that share `LastDictationSessionCache` now use its non-parallel collection so their ownership evidence is deterministic.

Final size is 1,723 lines for `TextboxWorkbenchWindow.xaml.cs` and 336 lines for `TextboxWorkbenchWindow.xaml`. The code-behind remains above the normal below-500 guideline, but its remaining methods classify as WPF composition/lifetime, dialogs and local file/clipboard boundaries, event adapters, cross-view text/focus synchronization, typed-result rendering, theme/menu placement, or top-level presentation dispatch. No remaining business rule, persistence decision, operation/cancellation state, duplicated enablement/visibility calculation, or independently testable workflow was found in the window. Splitting those cohesive WPF adapters into partial/base/arbitrary helper files would change file shape without improving ownership, so UI-001 records an explicit size exception.

Focused automated evidence covers settings races/cancellation/fallback/recovery; history selection/provenance/cache/rename/reconciliation; dictation/chat/import/read-aloud ownership and cancellation; reducer busy/readiness precedence; quick-settings debounce/disposal; transient-status expiry/disposal; prompt synchronization/suppression/deferred disposal; sidebar selection/tooltip/accessibility markup; controller/view dependency guardrails; and the rule that Workbench workflow tests do not construct WPF controls. No manual UI, accessibility, locale, DPI, microphone, hotkey, model, or hardware result is claimed by this audit.
